# Blue needle-nose pliers identification

19 November 2026 · For review before Blender modelling

**Strong visual match. The long straight taper, rounded joint and slim blue grips follow Rapid’s 130 mm snipe-nose pliers. “Snipe nose” is the useful UK catalogue term.**

The second tool has straight, tapering jaws with flat gripping faces, a broad round pivot, a small spring immediately behind it, and slim blue sleeves that curve away from each other. The jaw-to-handle proportions and sleeve shape agree closely with the PC-022 photograph. Rapid specifies a lap joint, return spring, PVC handles and 130 mm overall length. The OOMP record independently uses the 130 mm nominal size. The photograph shows no legible maker’s mark. [Workshop photograph](../PXL_20261119_133308647.jpg) · [Existing OOMP record](working.yaml)

**Rapid PC-022** is the brand and model in Rapid’s listing. **85-0225** is Rapid’s order code. [Product record](https://www.rapidonline.com/rapid-pc-022-economy-mini-snipe-nose-plier-85-0225) · [Catalogue photograph](https://static.rapidonline.com/catalogueimages/product/85/02/s85-0225p01ws.jpg) · [2019 catalogue, p. 206](https://static.rapidonline.com/downloads/2019-edu/dt-hand-tools.pdf#page=4)

## What would settle the identification

For the most recognisable render, measure pivot centre to jaw tip and photograph the jaw faces and spring side-on. A close-up of either pivot face could settle the maker.
