# Red adjustable wire stripper identification

19 November 2026 · For review before Blender modelling

**Strong visual match. The jaw hole, straight graduated arm, slotted second arm and red grips line up. Rapid lists 130 mm overall. The OOMP record says 120 mm.**

The third tool shows a short hooked jaw with a small round hole, dark flat arms, a circular pivot, a graduated straight arm and an elongated slot carrying an adjustment screw. The two bright fasteners near the red sleeves are useful landmarks. Rapid’s Anvil photo shows the same arrangement. Its listing gives a spring return, adjustable gauge selector, PVC handles and 130 mm overall. The size in the existing OOMP name is 120 mm, and its geometry describes holder clearance. This leaves the physical length unresolved. [Workshop photograph](../PXL_20261119_133308647.jpg) · [Existing OOMP record](working.yaml)

**Anvil AV-LDWS** is the brand and model in Rapid’s listing. **86-0350** is Rapid’s order code. [Product record](https://www.rapidonline.com/anvil-av-ldws-light-duty-wire-stripper-86-0350) · [Catalogue photograph](https://static.rapidonline.com/catalogueimages/product/86/03/s86-0350p01ws.jpg) · [2019 catalogue, p. 205](https://static.rapidonline.com/downloads/2019-edu/dt-hand-tools.pdf#page=3)
