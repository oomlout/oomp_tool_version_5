# Black-and-yellow snap-off craft knife identification

19 November 2026 · For review before Blender modelling

**Likely STANLEY slide-lock family. The broad handle makes the 18 mm 0-10-151 the leading candidate. The similar 9 mm 0-10-150 is the size check.**

The bottom tool has a long black moulded front, a yellow rear section with a rounded heel, a yellow ridged thumb slide and a line of dark detents. A recessed wordmark is visible near the nose, but the letters and model number cannot be read reliably at this resolution. The shape and colour split point to STANLEY’s slide-lock snap-off family. The broad body and the existing 17 mm craft-knife label favour its 18 mm version. The 17 mm label came from the holder project and the photograph has no scale. “OLFA-style” and “Exacto-style” are workshop descriptions here. [Workshop photograph](../PXL_20261119_133308647.jpg) · [Existing OOMP record](working.yaml)

STANLEY lists **0-10-151** with an 18 mm blade and an ABS housing. Its detailed UK specification gives a body length of 165 mm, width of 30 mm and height of 15 mm. The related **0-10-150** takes a 9 mm blade and has a slimmer body. These are reference dimensions for the listed products. ## What would settle the identification

The blade-width marking on a matching refill pack, a legible moulded code on the handle, or the closed body dimensions will help settle the variant. A reverse-side photo would also establish the rear-cap contour.
