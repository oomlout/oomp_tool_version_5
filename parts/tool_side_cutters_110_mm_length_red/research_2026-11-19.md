# Red side cutters identification

19 November 2026 · For review before Blender modelling

**Strong visual match. The short silver jaws, looped return spring between the grips and broad curved red sleeves match the catalogue photograph. The 110 mm listing agrees with the holder record.**

The top tool has a bright metal head with a round pivot, a small rectangular feature behind the pivot, and a loop of wire between the handles. The red sleeves flare near the head and have rounded ends. Those features line up with Rapid’s product image. Rapid specifies a lap joint, PVC handles, return spring, separate wire-trapping feature and 110 mm overall length. The photograph does not resolve the stamp or the jaw-side guard clearly enough to identify an individual unit. [Workshop photograph](../PXL_20261119_133308647.jpg) · [Existing OOMP record](working.yaml)

**Rapid SA-706 RED** is the brand and model in Rapid’s listing. **86-0210** is Rapid’s order code. [Product record](https://www.rapidonline.com/rapid-sa-706-red-low-cost-side-cutters-wire-guard-86-0210) · [Catalogue photograph](https://static.rapidonline.com/catalogueimages/product/86/02/s86-0210p01ws.jpg) · [2019 catalogue, p. 205](https://static.rapidonline.com/downloads/2019-edu/dt-hand-tools.pdf#page=3)

## What would settle the identification

A close-up of the pivot stamp and the reverse face of the jaws would distinguish this cutter from a similar unbranded pressing. A closed tip-to-grip length would confirm the scale.
